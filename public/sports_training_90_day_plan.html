Action Roadmap

verified Days 1–30: The Proof Engine

Momentum & Social Proof

Focus on capturing the hard work and turning it into a "digital billboard" for parents.

Raw Training Clips (Daily)

Film 10-15 seconds of athletes mid-drill. Post as Stories/Reels. Parents LOVE seeing their kids work hard and will share these instantly to their own followers.

Google Review Sprint

Text every current parent a direct link. Aim for 10+ reviews. This is the #1 factor for ranking "near me" searches for free.

Weekly "Athlete Spotlight"

Highlight one athlete's progress (speed improvement or skill win). Tag the parent. It creates an emotional connection and reaches their entire social circle.

💡

Pro Tip: Don't worry about high-end video gear. "Raw" and "authentic" training footage converts better for local sports programs than polished ads.

hub Days 31–60: Local Network Dominance

The Referral Flywheel

Infiltrate the local conversations where parents already spend their time.

Facebook Community Presence

Join Hilton/Parma youth sports groups. Don't "sell"—just help. When a parent asks about training, respond with high value: "We see this a lot in our sessions, here is a drill we use..."

"Bring a Teammate" Week

Announce a specific week where current athletes can bring a friend for free. This doubles your "leads" in one week with zero ad spend.

League Partnership Outreach

Offer a "Free Skills Clinic" to a local team (Hilton Raiders, etc.). One hour of your time can secure 10+ seasonal enrollments.

🚀

The Math: If 10 athletes bring 1 friend each, and only 30% sign up, you've added 3 recurring clients ($375-$600/mo) for zero cost.

payments Days 61–90: High-Value Conversion

Scaling the Program

Shift from "getting clients" to "managing a waiting list" through scarcity.

Lean into Scarcity

Start posting "X spots left for the 5-week program." When parents see a group is nearly full, they stop "thinking about it" and start "buying it."

Middle/High School Coach Bridge

Direct outreach to school coaches. Position yourself as the "Offseason Prep Partner." One coach recommendation can fill an entire group session overnight.

The "Season-Ready" Push

Create a countdown to football tryouts or season starts. Market the 5-week program as the "Competitive Edge" they need to make the starting roster.
